# BOB SafePay AI 🛡️
> Next-Generation AI Digital Payment Security & Fraud Prevention System

## 🌟 What is BOB SafePay AI?
BOB SafePay AI is a digital payment defense platform designed for modern banking. It analyzes transactions in real time, flags suspicious payments (Safe, Review, High Risk), and features **SafePay AI**, a smart assistant that explains to customers exactly why a transaction was flagged.

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Real-Time Risk Scoring (0–100)**: Instant classification of payments.
- **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Tells you why a payment was marked risky (new device, weird location, midnight hour).
- **SafePay AI Chatbot**: A real context-aware assistant connected to backend AI.
- **Cybersecurity Dark UI**: Premium black and purple glowing design.
